Hryngar Retainer

Hryngar Retainer

Creature 3

Weak, reflavored Clockwork Fabricator

HP 35. Use the weak level-3 values from the finalized custom reference—not the normal level-4 Clockwork Fabricator.

Runic heart. A Retainer becomes inert at 0 HP or when its runic heart is disabled. If Barrow Pulse reaches an exposed inert Retainer, it regains 10 HP and reactivates. A solid column blocks this effect.

Tactics. Use the chain launcher, Pull, Grab, and Constrict to expose characters to the next Barrow Pulse.
