Barrow Pulse

Barrow Pulse

Hazard 4

Unique · Complex · Magical

A wave of sepulchral force radiates from the sealed casket, restoring its runic servants while punishing anything caught in the open.

Encounter rules. A harmless Warning Pulse establishes line of effect. During combat, the pulse originates at the casket. An inert exposed Retainer struck by it regains 10 HP and reactivates; a Retainer fully behind a solid column remains inert. Once all three Retainers are inert at the same time, the Runelord emerges and becomes the pulse origin. Destroying the Runelord ends the hazard.

Cover. Solid columns completely block the pulse.
