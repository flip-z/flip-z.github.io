# Chapter 2: Grand Mausoleum
{: tabindex="-1" }

<blockquote class="read-aloud">The big stone door opens, catching and grinding over eons of dust and bone. The room beyond is slowly revealed by the dim light behind you and seems devoid of the decay you have grown accustomed to down here. Your first steps echo throughout a chamber much larger than the one you left. As your eyes adjust, crystal-blue braziers crackle to life through the hall, revealing the full grandeur of the marble mausoleum. Great pillars line its center. At the far end, a few short stairs lead up to a pristine marble sarcophagus.</blockquote>

## Foreshadow the pulse

Before anything activates, describe scorched sightlines radiating from the casket, clean shadows behind the solid columns, funerary chains connected to three linked seals, and faint runes in the Retainers’ chests. Let players inspect and reposition freely. Solid columns completely block the pulse.

## Final encounter sequence

1. Touching the casket releases a harmless **Warning Pulse**. Resolve its line of effect and explicitly show that a column blocks it.
2. Three weak, reflavored Clockwork Fabricators—the **[Hryngar Retainers]({{ '/adventures/breach-below-bergum/gm/bestiary/hryngar-retainer/' | relative_url }})**—awaken. Roll initiative.
3. **[Barrow Pulse]({{ '/adventures/breach-below-bergum/gm/bestiary/barrow-pulse/' | relative_url }})** activates each round from the casket.
4. A Retainer becomes inert at 0 HP or when its runic heart is disabled.
5. A pulse that reaches an inert Retainer restores 10 HP and reactivates it. A solid column prevents this.
6. When all three Retainers are inert simultaneously, the three seals break and the **[Reanimated Hryngar Runelord]({{ '/adventures/breach-below-bergum/gm/bestiary/reanimated-hryngar-runelord/' | relative_url }})** emerges.
7. Barrow Pulse transfers to the Runelord and originates from its current space.
8. The party can focus on the Runelord, control reactivated Retainers, or position inert Retainers safely behind columns.
9. Destroying the Runelord ends the pulse.

## Retainer tactics

Retainers use their chain launchers to threaten characters outside cover, then **Pull**, **Grab**, and **Constrict** to drag targets into the next pulse’s line. They value exposing a target over dealing immediate damage. Telegraph this intent by having chains scrape across column edges and Retainers reposition for a clear lane.

## Running the state changes

Use three visible tokens for Retainer state and flip each between active and inert. At the start of every pulse, trace line of effect from its origin to every creature and inert Retainer. Announce when all three seals break. Once the Runelord appears, physically move the pulse-origin marker with it.
