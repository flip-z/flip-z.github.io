# Chapter 1: Into the Crypt
{: tabindex="-1" }

## Opening

<blockquote class="read-aloud">The mischief of Bergum fades out above you as you enter the lower shaft behind the Sheriff. It’s quiet in a way you might expect of a cold, dank cave, but all around are the remnants of quite a commotion. Discarded picks and shovels sit silently against the rock, joined by ousted lanterns that have already seen their final flames.<br><br>As you branch from the main tunnels into a cramped, fresh alcove, the Sheriff stops abruptly, hangs his lantern on a thick steel hook, and beckons you close.<br><br>“Alright. Breach is right around this corner. The whole shaft’s been empty since the night it opened. Barely anyone’ll set foot down here these last few days, and I don’t blame ’em. Can’t say I’m too confident you’ll be able to seal this up, but I guess you’re all we’ve got right now. You handle what’s in there, and I’ll keep things from fallin’ apart topside.”<br><br>He lights a fresh torch and heads back up the way you came.</blockquote>

If the players are new to Pathfinder, invite character introductions and a quick review of their options here. The sheriff and deputies have kept curious townsfolk away, so this is the first willing expedition. No one topside has useful details about the tomb.

<section class="room-entry">
## A1. Entrance

<blockquote class="read-aloud">The tunnel ends abruptly at a wall of collapsed rubble—the site of the miners’ catastrophic mistake. As you scramble into the opening, the cramped tunnel gives way to a massive, cavernous hall of the dead. The air is stagnant and carries a tang of cave moss and a thousand years of decay.<br><br>Dozens of stone sarcophagi line the floor, but the peace of this place has been shattered. Mining tremors cracked the seals on many tombs, and faint, cold mist drifts from open caskets, confirming this as the source of the town’s haunting.<br><br>Across the expanse of stone and shadow, a single narrow door stands ajar, leading deeper into darkness.</blockquote>

**GM description.** Establish the scale, decay, and disturbed seals. The route onward is the narrow door.

**Connections and consequences.** A2 is within this burial hall. The open door leads to A3.
</section>

<section class="room-entry">
## A2. Disturbed Burial

**Read aloud after the sarcophagus is noticed:**

<blockquote class="read-aloud">It didn’t crumble like the wall, but a jagged spiderweb fracture runs down the sarcophagus. Through the fissure comes a faint, dry scratching—like fingernails on stone.</blockquote>

**Checks and discoveries.** A **DC 15 Seek** check identifies the cracked sarcophagus near the entrance.

**Hazard.** Interacting with the damaged tomb risks the **Entombed Spirit**. See the [hazard index]({{ '/adventures/breach-below-bergum/gm/hazards/#entombed-spirit' | relative_url }}).

**Casket cover.** AC 15, Hardness 5; **DC 18 Athletics** to move.

**Reward.** Breaking open or moving the cover reveals a corpse wearing a shattered amulet. Its setting holds a fist-sized deep-blue crystal crossed by pale fractures. It feels unnaturally cold and pulses faintly.

**Connections and consequences.** The blue crystal is one of three that can bypass the A7 guardians.
</section>

<section class="room-entry">
## A3. Dusty Hallway

<blockquote class="read-aloud">Pushing through the narrow door, the atmosphere instantly changes from vault-like crypt to choking hallway. The stone walls are slick with humidity and the ceiling presses low overhead. Deep, dusty alcoves line both sides, holding dozens of funerary urns—some clay, some bronze—all covered in soot and dust.</blockquote>

**Hazard and creatures.** Reaching into a large urn on the north side triggers **Hampering Web**, followed by a **Spider Swarm**. See the [hazard index]({{ '/adventures/breach-below-bergum/gm/hazards/#hampering-web' | relative_url }}).

**Reward.** The urn cache contains **48 gp**, a **lesser Mistform Elixir**, and a **Feather Token (bird)**.

**Connections and consequences.** The hall continues toward A5. Rubble to the right offers a route through A4.
</section>

<section class="room-entry">
## A4. Collapsed Wall

<blockquote class="read-aloud">The hallway curves into pitch darkness, but to your right a precarious mound of collapsed rubble has opened the wall, offering a dangerous path over the wreckage into another chamber.</blockquote>

**Checks and discoveries.**

- **DC 8 Athletics** to Climb the wreckage.
- At the top, **DC 12 Acrobatics** to Balance across shifting debris.
- After **three total failures** across these checks, the passage seals for the rest of the party.

**Connections and consequences.** This is an alternate route around the dark hallway. Anyone isolated by the collapse reunites with the group in A7.
</section>

<section class="room-entry">
## A5. Dark Hallway

<blockquote class="read-aloud">The hallway continues forward, narrow and draped in complete darkness. Its smooth stone walls are cool and damp, their torchlight long faded.</blockquote>

**Hazards.** The first character to pass the collapsed wall is afflicted by **Phantom Footsteps**. Near the corner, a character without darkvision or light trips over a skeleton and triggers **Pit of Toil**. See the [hazard index]({{ '/adventures/breach-below-bergum/gm/hazards/#phantom-footsteps' | relative_url }}).

**Checks and discoveries.** A character with darkvision or a light source must Seek the skeleton to notice what it holds.

**Reward.** Clutched in the skeleton’s hand is a heavy crimson crystal with a faint internal glimmer.

**Connections and consequences.** The crimson crystal is the second key for A7.
</section>

<section class="room-entry">
## A6. Treasure Room

<blockquote class="read-aloud">Along the Atrium’s western wall, ten feet of smooth stone appears perfectly seamless. An exceptionally observant eye might catch one flaw: near the center, a small block protrudes barely a finger-width, betrayed by a ridge of settled dust.<br><br>Pushing it sends a low grinding shudder through the floor. The wall slides into darkness, revealing a small, dry chamber where torchlight catches the gleam of gold and treasures.</blockquote>

**Checks and discoveries.** The PDF does not assign a discovery DC. Use the party’s exploration activities and Pathfinder’s level-appropriate simple DC guidance rather than hiding required progress behind an invented number.

**Reward.** The main chest holds the final yellow-amber crystal, clouded with immobile black motes, plus **300 gp**, an **Iron Cube**, a **Traveler’s Any-Tool**, and **two lesser Elixirs of Life**.

**Connections and consequences.** The amber crystal completes the set used at A7.
</section>

<section class="room-entry">
## A7. Atrium

<blockquote class="read-aloud">The cramped passage gives way to a large square chamber of impressive vaulted stone. It feels grand and solemn, dominated by stone gargoyles set into all four walls, staring down with vacant, malevolent eyes.<br><br>Along the far wall, a high mound of rubble reveals the other side of the earlier cave-in. The eastern wall holds an elaborate door of massive granite slabs carved with forgotten runes. Beside it stands a heavy stone lever.</blockquote>

**Checks and discoveries.** A secret **DC 5 Perception** check notices three shaped crystal sockets above the lever.

**Creatures.** Pulling the lever without all three crystals activates **four Gargoyles**. Use the published [Gargoyle statistics on Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3001). The door opens when they are defeated.

**Bypass.** Placing the blue, crimson, and amber crystals in the sockets causes the gargoyles to crumble and opens the door without combat.

**Connections and consequences.** The door leads to the Grand Mausoleum.
</section>
