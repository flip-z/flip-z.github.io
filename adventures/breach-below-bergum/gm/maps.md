# Dungeon Map
{: tabindex="-1" }

The source map is unlabeled. Use this key with the preview:

- **A1–A2:** Large burial hall at upper left, including the disturbed sarcophagus
- **A3:** Curving corridor around the burial hall
- **A4:** Central rubble breach
- **A5:** Lower dark hallway
- **A6:** Small secret room off the atrium approach
- **A7:** Transition space before the blue door
- **Grand Mausoleum:** Bright chamber on the right, with solid columns and the sarcophagus
