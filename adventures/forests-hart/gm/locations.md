# Locations
{: tabindex="-1" }

## Coedmyr village

The investigation hub. Important functions include Mayor Elara Venn's office, the Pilgrim's Rest, Maelin Tor's home, Taran Holt's forge, local hunters, and ordinary townsfolk with conflicting beliefs about the Hart.

Malformed wildlife can attack near town at dusk early in the investigation. If the party later frees the Hart without resolving its condition, a second and more severe attack can demonstrate that the mobile corruption is worsening.

## Pilgrim's Rest

A large inn left over from Coedmyr's busier pilgrimage era. It has more rooms and tables than the modern village can support. The common room is useful for informal conversations, rumors, and watching villagers react to the Ministry and the unusually rare sight of an elf.

Ewan can return here after the trap-line investigation. If the party later recognizes the need for a bellkeeper, the inn provides a natural place to find and persuade him without making recruitment a mandatory quest.

## Maelin Tor's home

Maelin lives with or is regularly cared for by his granddaughter Nessa, who serves as the party's primary conversational contact. The house is crowded with books, hunting pieces, pilgrimage keepsakes, bones, carved animals, iron charms, and old hanging ornaments.

The Wolf's Tooth is hidden in plain sight among this clutter rather than displayed as an obvious relic. Maelin preserves archaic vocabulary about the Hart, Iron-Maw, mercy, straying, and appointed ends, but does not provide procedural knowledge of the old rite.

## Taran Holt's forge

Provides the material recognition and reconstruction path for the Wolf's Tooth. Taran is skeptical and practical. He can identify the relic as forged cold iron designed to mount on a haft and show the party a mundane analogue of the obsolete regional implement.

Once the party has evidence that the Tooth activates the shrine, Taran can supervise a short reconstruction challenge in which the PCs build the ceremonial hunting weapon themselves.

## Pilgrimage overlook

Sera Wynn tends what remains of the old pilgrimage site. The overlook contains a poorly catalogued archive with records on Coedmyr hunting customs, Iron-Maw, the Hart, traditional tools, spiritual hunts, the spirit trail, and the forgotten shrine.

The archive corroborates Maelin's oral tradition and supplies historical and geographic clues without directly revealing that the Hart must die.

## Spirit trail

An old route through the forest connecting the pilgrimage overlook to the ruined hunting shrine. Weathered wolf carvings mark the path. Careful exploration can reveal it even without complete documentary directions.

## Ewan Rusk's trap line

Local game has become scarce enough that Ewan has neglected distant parts of the line.

The party first encounters a trapped or recently killed doe whose impossible healing demonstrates the underlying pathology: damaged life continues accumulating instead of reaching a normal endpoint.

Farther along the line lies the trapped Hart, surrounded by a dangerous aura of uncontrolled vitality. The Hart is the concentrated source of the immediate outbreak. Ordinary attacks cannot complete its death, and close approach exposes living creatures to the same uncontrolled growth.

Freeing the Hart is valid but makes the source mobile and intensifies subsequent outbreaks.

## Corrupted forest

Contains malformed fauna, contaminated waterways, evidence of the long ecological decline, and the routes used during the final pursuit.

The corruption should consistently resemble uncontrolled or misdirected life rather than generic decay: redundant growth, wounds that refuse to resolve, tissue accumulating around damage, and vegetation continuing past healthy limits.

## Ruined hunting shrine

The final ritual destination. Clearing growth and debris exposes the bell and carvings showing distinct bellkeeper and hunter roles.

An ordinary bell strike produces only a momentary pale aura. The Wolf's Tooth produces the correct resonance, but the aura must be maintained through repeated ringing.

During the final rite, the active boundary excludes ordinary corrupted wildlife while allowing the called Hart to enter. Inside the aura, corruption is driven out of the Hart and suspended visibly around the shrine perimeter rather than destroyed. Interrupting the bell allows that corruption to press back inward and creates openings for malformed creatures to breach the boundary.
