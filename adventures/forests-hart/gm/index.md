# Forest's Hart: GM Guide
{: tabindex="-1" }

## Adventure premise

The party arrives to investigate a failing village and an impossible wildlife outbreak. They discover that the legendary Hart, a spiritual force of life and renewal, has been injured and trapped inside a community whose old stewardship system has collapsed. Its vitalizing nature has become a genuine corrupting force.

The investigation should lead the party toward an attempted rescue. Coedmyr's surviving traditions frame four forces that once remained in relationship: the Hart as life, the Wolf as death, the Bird as change, and the River Turtle as tradition. The party reconstructs enough of that system to suppress and stabilize the corruption, but the finale reveals that this Hart manifestation is too far gone to restore with the surviving practices.

The final decision is not prescribed by an old ritual. The party must decide whether to use the Wolf's Tooth, a tool of limits and restraint, to kill the Hart and save Coedmyr despite not knowing the long-term spiritual consequence.

## At a glance

- **System:** Pathfinder Second Edition
- **Shape:** 2–3 session investigation sandbox
- **Core loop:** investigate Coedmyr → reconstruct stewardship practices → find and try to save the Hart → failed restoration becomes pursuit → make the final choice
- **Finale:** attempted rescue, improvised pursuit, containment, village conflict, and a lethal choice without a known correct spiritual answer

## Current required preparation

The reconstructed Wolf's Tooth and discovery of the shrine are the intended hard gates. Turtle- and Bird-associated tools or practices, local support, Hart-source knowledge, and archive research provide advantages without becoming mandatory collectibles.
