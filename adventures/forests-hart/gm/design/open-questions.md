# Open Design Questions
{: tabindex="-1" }

1. Is the current outbreak directly caused by the skeptical hunter trapping or wounding the Hart, and is that fact initially concealed?
2. What exact opening challenge in FH-00 demonstrates corrupted animals and vegetation without revealing the Hart too early?
3. Which two or three concrete symptoms define Coedmyr's current crisis, and how recently did they begin?
4. Which named villagers represent the main belief positions: skeptic, protector-believer, and old-tradition believer?
5. Was the original "cooky believer" intended to be an actual cook or simply a kooky old believer? The current files canonized him as the old cook.
6. Why does the relic keeper trust the Ministry enough to hand over the Wolf's Tooth, and what exact archaic phrase allows the party to interpret its purpose as purification before understanding its older meaning?
7. What physical evidence lets the blacksmith recognize the Wolf's Tooth as a weapon component, and exactly what must the PCs fabricate around it?
8. How do PCs meaningfully participate in crafting without making one Crafting check a hard gate? What materials, time, cost, and alternate skills apply?
9. How many independent clue paths point to the ruined shrine so that exploration order cannot stall the adventure?
10. Does the shrine need actual restoration before the bell works, or is discovering and correctly using the bell enough?
11. What exact mechanical information does an incorrect bell strike reveal: aura radius, duration, effect on corruption, and evidence of the separate bellkeeper role?
12. Does FH-04 begin with the Hart caught in the hunter's trap, making the first instinctive objective to rescue it, or is the Hart already loose when first encountered?
13. What is the objective and exit condition of the first Hart encounter so it teaches rather than becoming an unwinnable boss fight?
14. If the party lets the hunter go alone, what exact information returns with him so that choice is different but not punitive?
15. What visible behavior proves that pursuit and exhaustion matter rather than leaving that conclusion to GM explanation?
16. What exact combination of clues establishes the truth that the old rite is a ritual hunt? The reveal should not depend on a single NPC or check.
17. Who is the preferred bellkeeper candidate, and what proof changes that person's mind enough to participate?
18. How much training does the bellkeeper actually require, and should training ever fail once the party has correctly reconstructed the rite?
19. If no NPC bellkeeper is recruited, does one PC remain at the shrine for the entire pursuit rather than merely paying a recurring action cost?
20. Why is there still a chase if the bell calls the Hart toward a known destination? Does the bell call it onto an ancestral route that must be completed and exhausted before it reaches the shrine?
21. How many chase obstacles are used, how is difficulty adjusted for a three-character pursuit, and how are chase failures converted into Hart exhaustion or shrine-phase pressure?
22. Which four or five concrete chase obstacles best support Athletics, Survival, attacks, spells, and investigative skills without becoming four repeated skill checks?
23. What does chase performance materially change when the Hart reaches the shrine: HP, defenses, actions, remaining corruption, side creatures, or some combination?
24. What exact state makes the Hart ready for the Wolf's Tooth at the shrine, and does the final ritual action require an attack roll or become automatic once the party has earned the opening?
25. Does the active shrine fully exclude corrupted side creatures or merely weaken them?
26. What constitutes final failure: the bell stopping, the Hart reaching the shrine insufficiently exhausted, the party retreating, or a conventional combat loss?
27. What observable evidence makes continued healing or refusal to complete the rite a clear choice rather than a hidden gotcha?
28. What does success actually leave behind for Coedmyr once the Hart's miraculous era ends, and how quickly does failure spread beyond the village?
29. Is level 10 locked for the Forest's Hart pregens?
30. The current character data only persists the Order of Twilight druid. Which previously discussed martial pregens should be restored as the other three canonical Ministry investigators?
31. Which PC owns the strongest Crafting, Survival, social, and investigation coverage so the sandbox does not accidentally make the druid solve every clue?
32. What does the Twilight druid know automatically on entering the region, and what must still be learned through investigation so the subclass theme does not reveal the mystery immediately?
33. What individual motive or bias should each PC bring to the question of saving versus ending the Hart's liminal state?
34. What are the names, relationships, and private stakes of the relic keeper, blacksmith, hunter, and village authority?
