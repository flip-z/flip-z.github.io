# Open Design Questions
{: tabindex="-1" }

Answered decisions now live in the canonical data files. Remaining questions:

1. What opening challenge introduces malformed fauna, failed crops, and contaminated water without revealing the Hart too early?
2. Why does Maelin Tor hand over the Wolf's Tooth, and what exact archaic phrase communicates mercy without revealing the final answer?
3. What exact ceremonial implement does Taran Holt identify, and what pieces besides the Wolf's Tooth must be fabricated?
4. What is the reconstruction procedure: useful skills, DCs, duration, materials, and how overlook research helps without making one failed check a hard gate?
5. What exact information can the party identify from the momentary weak bell aura?
6. What is the objective and end condition of the first scene at Ewan's trap line, including the branches where the Hart is freed early or left in place?
7. What evidence convinces Ewan or another villager to serve as bellkeeper, and does bellkeeper training require a check?
8. How many pursuit obstacles make the finale substantial without overstaying it?
9. Which pursuit obstacles give Athletics, Survival, magic, observation, and investigation distinct useful approaches?
10. How should the pursuit change when only three PCs participate because one remained as bellkeeper?
11. What are the exact breach-pressure thresholds, and how does each threshold alter the shrine phase?
12. What exact event counts as a bellkeeper interruption?
13. What are the shrine aura's radius and mechanical effects?
14. What level and encounter budget should the Hart and shrine complications use for four level-10 PCs?
15. What mechanically makes the Hart exposed and enables Complete the Passage?
16. What evidence makes continued attempts to preserve the Hart clearly ineffective rather than a hidden gotcha?
17. What exactly happens to Coedmyr after success, and what larger regional consequence follows failure?
18. Which martial class best fills the fourth PC slot: Fighter, Ranger, Guardian, or another option?
19. What are the final ancestry, background, skills, feats, equipment, and motivations for all four pregens?
20. What personal bias does each PC bring to preserving versus completing the Hart's natural passage?
