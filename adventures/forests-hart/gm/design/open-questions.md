# Open Design Questions
{: tabindex="-1" }

Answered decisions now live in the canonical data files. Remaining questions:

1. Does the Wolf need a regional proper name, or should Coedmyr simply call the continent-wide figure the Wolf?
2. What exact ceremonial implement does Taran Holt identify, and what pieces besides the Wolf's Tooth must be fabricated?
3. What exact information can the party identify from the momentary weak bell aura before the sustained rite is attempted?
4. How much unrelated Turtle, Bird, and obsolete religious lore should Maelin, Nessa, and the overlook contain before it becomes distracting rather than world-building?
5. What was the formal title of Coedmyr's lost practical religious keeper or warden, and how much of that institution should survive in Sera's modern role?
6. Which spirit-trail forest hooks should be canonical examples: abnormal animal cries, malformed tracks, abandoned traps, or a smaller curated set?
7. How should each optional bellkeeper differ mechanically, if at all, rather than only fictionally?
8. How many pursuit obstacles make the finale substantial without overstaying it?
9. Which pursuit obstacles give Athletics, Survival, magic, observation, and investigation distinct useful approaches?
10. How should the pursuit change when only three PCs participate because one remained as bellkeeper?
11. What are the exact breach-pressure thresholds, and how does each threshold alter the shrine phase?
12. What exact event counts as a bellkeeper interruption?
13. What are the shrine aura's radius and mechanical effects?
14. What level and encounter budget should the Hart and shrine complications use for four level-10 PCs?
15. What exact objectives represent separating the Hart from the surrounding disjunction during the shrine phase?
16. What mechanically makes the Hart coherent, lucid, and exposed and enables Complete the Passage?
17. What exact brief communication can the lucid Hart provide without turning into a procedural lore dump?
18. What evidence makes continued attempts to preserve the exposed Hart clearly ineffective rather than a hidden gotcha?
19. What exactly happens to Coedmyr after success, and what larger regional consequence follows failure?
20. What are the final ancestry, background, skills, feats, equipment, motivations, and names for all four pregens?
21. What personal bias does each PC bring to preserving versus completing the Hart manifestation's passage?
