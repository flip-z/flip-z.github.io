# Adventure Structure
{: tabindex="-1" }

## FH-00 — Ministry Arrival

Mayor Elara Venn has called the Ministry after three problems accelerated together: malformed aggressive wildlife attacking near town, crop failure, and a tainted water supply. The party is sent to determine whether they share a cause. They do not initially know about the Hart.

The Order of Twilight druid can quickly recognize that the apparent void influence is actually local life distorted by an imbalance in the natural cycle. That points the investigation toward a spiritual cause without revealing the old rite.

## FH-01 — Maelin Tor and the Wolf's Tooth

Maelin Tor is an elderly sage whose knowledge of old Coedmyr traditions is better than his ability to explain them. Villagers tend to dismiss him as a kook. He keeps a supposed cold-iron tooth of the mythical Iron-Maw, the great wolf invoked by old hunters to guide spiritual hunts.

Maelin describes the relic using archaic language about mercy, purification, and returning what has strayed to its appointed end. He provides the Wolf's Tooth and one independent clue toward the old rite.

## FH-02 — Taran Holt's Smithy

Taran Holt is a practical skeptic. He recognizes that the Wolf's Tooth is cold iron and was deliberately shaped to mount on a haft. Its form resembles an obsolete ceremonial Coedmyr polearm or mace head, and he can show the party a mundane completed analogue.

Taran will assist with reconstructing the implement once the party earns his cooperation through ordinary help or practical trust. Research in the overlook library about traditional regional tools makes the reconstruction easier.

## FH-03 — Overlook, Spirit Trail, and Shrine

Sera Wynn, Maelin Tor, the overlook library, or direct exploration of the spirit trail can each point toward the old shrine.

The shrine does not require a separate restoration quest. The party clears enough growth and debris to traverse it, expose the bell, and study the carvings. Striking the bell with an ordinary object produces a sub-round pale aura that briefly suppresses nearby corruption, then collapses. The carvings distinguish the bellkeeper from the hunters and preserve another clue about the old spiritual hunt.

## FH-04 — Ewan Rusk's Trap Line

Ewan Rusk is an experienced hunter who has stopped checking parts of his trap line because local game has dwindled along with the land. He does not know the Hart reached one of those unattended traps or that this event corresponds with the recent escalation.

Following the trap line leads the party to the Hart and the source of the immediate outbreak. They can test ordinary approaches, free the Hart, or leave it at the known location while they prepare the old rite. Ordinary intervention does not resolve its liminal condition.

Freeing the Hart early is valid. It simply means the party must locate it again when they are ready to begin the final pursuit.

## FH-05 — Recruit a Bellkeeper

Once the party understands that the bell must be maintained during the hunt, they can recruit any competent villager willing to accept the reconstructed rite. Ewan is the most natural candidate after confronting the consequences of his neglected trap line.

Without a helper, one PC remains at the shrine while the other three pursue the Hart.

## Investigation Reveal

The party understands the old rite after connecting any two of three independent clue categories:

1. Maelin's archaic language about mercy and an appointed end.
2. Taran's identification of the Wolf's Tooth as part of a traditional weapon-like implement.
3. Shrine or library records describing the old spiritual hunt.

This reveals the central reversal before the finale: the Hart is not meant to be preserved indefinitely. The forgotten rite completes its natural passage.

## FH-FINAL — Ritual Hunt

The reconstructed Wolf's Tooth strikes the shrine bell and produces a sustained aura. The bell calls the Hart toward the shrine but does not compel it there. The party must keep contact and drive it along the old hunt path.

Use a modified chase structure. Failed obstacles increase **breach pressure**: corruption and malformed fauna from the Hart's wake reach the bellkeeper ahead of the party. If the Hart was freed earlier, reacquiring it adds an additional obstacle.

When the party reaches the shrine, breach pressure determines how unstable the encounter begins. The party protects the active bell, resolves accumulated complications, and uses the aura to expose the Hart beneath its remaining liminal corruption.

Once the Hart is exposed inside the active aura, a character adjacent to it with the Wolf's Tooth can spend 1 action to **Complete the Passage**. No final check is required; reaching that state is the encounter's challenge.

If the party is defeated or abandons the active rite, the corruption continues to worsen and may damage the shrine, creating a larger regional threat.
